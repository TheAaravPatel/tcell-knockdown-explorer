    About this tool

    What is Perturb-seq?

    Scientists switch off one gene in a cell, then read out the activity (RNA levels) of thousands of other genes in that single cell. Doing this for every gene, across millions of cells, maps which genes control which.

    What does CRISPRi knockdown mean?

    CRISPR interference uses a "dead" Cas9 that cannot cut DNA. It is guided to a gene's start and sits there, turning the gene down (not fully off). The tool shows how strongly each gene was actually reduced.

    What do the colors mean?

    Blue genes went down after the knockdown; red genes went up. Effect sizes are log₂ fold changes: −1 means halved, +1 means doubled. Only genes with adjusted p-value < 0.05 are shown (top 200 per gene).

    What does the chain-reaction model assume?

    If gene A lowers gene B, and we know what knocking down B does, then B passes on a proportional share of its own effects: share = (change in B) ÷ (B's own knockdown). Effects add up along paths and are capped. It assumes effects are linear and additive, ignores genes never knocked down in the screen, and can double-count because measured effects already contain indirect ones.

    Cell conditions

    Rest: resting T cells. Stim 8hr / 48hr: T cells 8 or 48 hours after activation (as when fighting an infection). A key finding of the paper is that many regulators act very differently in resting vs. activated cells.
